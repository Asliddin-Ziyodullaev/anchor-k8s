# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Anchor K8s community welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss code, evidence and behavior rather than attacking people.
- Assume good intent and ask before assuming.
- Respect privacy: never post someone's kubeconfig, tokens, cluster addresses or other private data.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about scope and moderation gracefully.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact information on the [project's GitHub page](https://github.com/anchor-k8s). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
